About COAST

South Florida sits at the leading edge of coupled coastal Earth-system change: intensifying tropical cyclones, marine heatwaves, accelerating sea-level rise, reef decline, and the solid-Earth response to ocean and storm loading. COAST uses this region and the broader tropical western Atlantic as a living laboratory.

The question behind every COAST project

How do coastal Earth systems respond to environmental forcing and extreme events, and how can we observe, model, and understand those responses to inform a more resilient future?

The Rosenstiel School is one of very few places in the United States where atmospheric, ocean, marine-biology, environmental, and geoscience faculty work side by side on a single island campus, across five departments. The school houses the NOAA Cooperative Institute for Marine and Atmospheric Studies (CIMAS), sits directly across the street from NOAA's Atlantic Oceanographic and Meteorological Laboratory (AOML), and is near the U.S. National Hurricane Center.

Ocean

Currents, chemistry, and life at sea

Physical oceanography, ocean biogeochemistry and carbon cycling, marine ecology, and autonomous and ship-based observing.

Atmosphere

Weather, climate, and air-sea exchange

Boundary-layer dynamics, aerosol chemistry, climate modeling, and machine-learning approaches to weather and climate.

Solid Earth

Coasts, sediments, and the seafloor

Seismology and geophysical imaging, carbonate sedimentology, and paleoclimate and sea-level reconstruction.

Technology

Sensing & computing infrastructure

The R/V F. G. Walton Smith, the SUSTAIN wind-wave-surge laboratory, CSTARS remote sensing, genomics, and high-performance computing.

Participating Departments

COAST projects span all five departments of the Rosenstiel School, so whichever one your mentor sits in, you spend the summer inside a school-wide research environment.

ATM
Atmospheric Sciences
Tropical meteorology and hurricanes, climate dynamics and prediction, clouds and aerosols, and atmospheric chemistry
EVR
Environmental Science & Policy
Environmental policy and sustainability, climate risk and preparedness, conservation biology, and fisheries science
MBE
Marine Biology & Ecology
Marine ecology, physiology, genomics, behavior, fisheries, toxicology, and conservation science
MGS
Marine Geosciences
Geophysics and natural hazards, carbonate sedimentology and reefs, paleoclimate and geochemistry, and sea-level science
OCE
Ocean Sciences
Physical oceanography and ocean dynamics, air-sea interaction, biogeochemistry, and ocean observing and engineering
