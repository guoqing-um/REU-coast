Applications

How to Apply

Applications are submitted through NSF ETAP. There is no application fee, and prior research experience is not required.

COAST welcomes applicants at all undergraduate stages, and especially encourages rising sophomores and juniors and community-college transfer students.

Apply through NSF ETAP

Confirm eligibility

Check the eligibility rules below before you start.

Prepare your materials

A personal statement, an academic transcript (unofficial accepted), one faculty reference, and your ranked preferences among the COAST projects.

Submit through NSF ETAP

Applications are accepted only through the portal, not by email.

Review and project matching

The program team reads every application, and faculty mentors weigh in on fit with their projects. Offers and project matches go out in late March, balancing your ranked preferences with mentor availability.

Arrive on Virginia Key

The program opens in mid-May with an orientation week: safety and heat-safety training, mentor pitches, and a campus tour of Rosenstiel and CIMAS.

Eligibility & Selection Criteria

Per NSF eligibility rules, applicants must be U.S. citizens, U.S. nationals, or U.S. permanent residents enrolled in an associate or baccalaureate degree program at the time of the summer experience, including eligible transfer and incoming students. Students who will have completed a bachelor's degree before the program begins are not eligible.

What we look for:

  • Motivation and curiosity
  • Preparation suited to the project you want
  • Potential for growth as a researcher
  • Alignment with the projects running that summer
  • Evidence of persistence, problem-solving, or teamwork

COAST is built for students whose home colleges do not offer research like this, and at least half of each cohort comes from such institutions, including community colleges. We recruit nationally: in a typical year the ten participants come from ten different institutions in several states.
