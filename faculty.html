Faculty Mentors

Faculty Mentors

Twenty faculty mentors across the five departments of the Rosenstiel School lead the COAST project pool. Each year's active set is drawn from this group.

The COAST mentor pool spans early-, mid-, and senior-career faculty across all five Rosenstiel departments, organized through 16 project groups led by 20 mentors. All mentors have prior undergraduate-mentoring experience, and each year the PI and Co-PI select the active mentor set to balance the four cross-cutting themes and the five departments. Several projects are co-led, pairing complementary expertise. Browse the projects each mentor leads on the Research page.

ATM

Atmospheric Sciences

Boundary-layer dynamics, aerosols, climate, and AI for weather
Paquita ZuidemaATM

Atmospheric boundary-layer structure, aerosols, and air-sea interaction; leads the Florida Atmospheric Boundary Layer Experiment (FABLE).

Siyuan WangATM

Aerosol thermodynamics and machine-learning emulators for integration into atmospheric-modeling workflows.

Marybeth ArcodiaATM

Explainable AI and climate variability; prediction of coastal marine heatwaves in the Caribbean-Florida-Gulf region.

Amy ClementATM

Large-scale climate dynamics and variability of the coupled ocean-atmosphere system.

Co-mentors the climate-risk and adaptation project (with K. Mach).

EVR

Environmental Science & Policy

Climate risk, hazard assessment, adaptation, and environmental health
Katharine MachEVR

Climate risk and adaptation science in partnership with local governments and community organizations; decision support.

Kilan Ashad-BishopEVR

Mixed-methods environmental-health research at the intersection of urban fishing, extreme heat, and heat-related health risk in Miami-Dade.

MBE

Marine Biology & Ecology

Coral reefs, fisheries, population genomics, and microbial ecology
Diego LirmanMBE

Coral-reef restoration ecology integrating field, laboratory, and citizen-science components.

Douglas CrawfordMBE

Population and functional genomics of coastal fishes and environmental adaptation.

Co-leads Biscayne Bay population genomics (with M. Oleksiak).

Marjorie OleksiakMBE

Population genomics and the genetic basis of adaptation to environmental change in fishes.

Co-leads Biscayne Bay population genomics (with D. Crawford).

Elizabeth BabcockMBE

Quantitative marine ecology applied to fisheries and ecosystem management; species-distribution and food-web modeling.

Mohammad MoniruzzamanMBE

Giant-virus impacts on coastal microbial food webs and biogeochemistry, using metagenomics and bioinformatics.

Rachael HeuerMBE

Effects of climate-related stressors and pollutants on neural and behavioral function in marine and model organisms.

MGS

Marine Geosciences

Seismology, sedimentology, paleoclimate, and geodynamics
Guoqing LinMGS

Seismology and dense-array imaging; shallow carbonate-platform structure and ocean-storm-solid-Earth coupling.

Adam HoltMGS

Computational geodynamics of subduction systems and the controls on tectonic plate motions.

Amanda OehlertMGS

Carbonate sedimentology and geochemistry; reconstructing past environments and sea level from sediment archives.

Co-leads the Mozambique reef-core project (with G. Eberli & R. Weger).

Gregor EberliMGS

Carbonate platforms, sea-level history, and the sedimentary record of environmental change.

Co-leads the Mozambique reef-core project (with A. Oehlert & R. Weger).

Ralf WegerMGS

Carbonate petrophysics and sediment-core analysis linking rock properties to depositional history.

Co-leads the Mozambique reef-core project (with G. Eberli & A. Oehlert).

Mariana BifOCE

Ocean carbon and nitrogen cycling using BGC-Argo floats, satellite remote sensing, and shipboard observations.
