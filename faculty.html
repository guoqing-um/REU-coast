Faculty Mentors

Faculty Mentors

Twenty faculty mentors across the five departments of the Rosenstiel School lead the COAST project pool. Each year's active set is drawn from this group.

The COAST mentor pool spans early-, mid-, and senior-career faculty across all five Rosenstiel departments, organized through 16 project groups led by 20 mentors. All mentors have prior undergraduate-mentoring experience, and each year the PI and Co-PI select the active mentor set to balance the four cross-cutting themes and the five departments. Several projects are co-led, pairing complementary expertise. Browse the projects each mentor leads on the Research page.

ATM

Atmospheric Sciences

Boundary-layer dynamics, aerosols, climate, and AI for weather
Paquita ZuidemaATM Professor & Chair, Atmospheric Sciences

Studies how clouds, tiny airborne particles, and the lowest layer of the atmosphere interact with the ocean surface, flying instruments through coastal skies to learn how the atmosphere shapes weather and climate. Leads the Florida Atmospheric Boundary Layer Experiment (FABLE).

UM profile
Siyuan WangATM Associate Professor, Atmospheric Sciences

Builds computer models of airborne particles, including wildfire smoke, and uses machine learning to capture how they affect air quality and climate.

UM profile
Marybeth ArcodiaATM Assistant Professor, Atmospheric Sciences & Frost Institute for Data Science and Computing

Uses explainable AI to understand and predict climate swings, including the marine heatwaves that strike the Caribbean, Florida, and Gulf coasts.

UM profile
Amy ClementATM Professor, Atmospheric Sciences

Investigates how the ocean and atmosphere work together to drive climate, from year-to-year shifts to long-term change. Co-mentors the climate-risk and adaptation project with K. Mach.

UM profile
EVR

Environmental Science & Policy

Climate risk, hazard assessment, adaptation, and environmental health
Katharine MachEVR Professor & Chair, Environmental Science & Policy

Assesses the risks a changing climate brings, from flooding and extreme heat to wildfire, and works with communities and governments to design fair, effective ways to adapt.

UM profile
Kilan Ashad-BishopEVR Assistant Professor, Environmental Science & Policy

Studies how social and environmental factors shape health in Miami neighborhoods, connecting urban fishing, extreme heat, and health disparities.

UM profile
MBE

Marine Biology & Ecology

Coral reefs, fisheries, population genomics, and microbial ecology
Diego LirmanMBE Associate Professor, Marine Biology & Ecology

Restores damaged coral reefs through hands-on fieldwork, lab experiments, and citizen science, including the Rescue a Reef program that brings volunteers into reef recovery.

UM profile
Douglas CrawfordMBE Professor, Marine Biology & Ecology

Explores how coastal fish adapt to changing environments by reading their genomes, work that also offers clues about human health. Co-leads Biscayne Bay population genomics with M. Oleksiak.

UM profile
Marjorie OleksiakMBE Professor, Marine Biology & Ecology

Uses genomics to understand how fish populations adapt, or fail to adapt, to environmental change. Co-leads Biscayne Bay population genomics with D. Crawford.

UM profile
Elizabeth BabcockMBE Professor, Marine Biology & Ecology; Associate Dean, Graduate Studies

Applies math and statistics to marine ecology, modeling fish populations and food webs to inform how fisheries and ecosystems are managed.

UM profile
Mohammad MoniruzzamanMBE Assistant Professor, Marine Biology & Ecology

Studies giant viruses, surprisingly complex microbes that shape ocean life, using genomics and computational biology to reveal how they interact with their hosts.

UM profile
Rachael HeuerMBE Associate Professor, Marine Biology & Ecology

Investigates how climate stressors such as ocean acidification and warming, along with pollutants like crude oil, affect the bodies and behavior of marine fish.

UM profile
MGS

Marine Geosciences

Seismology, sedimentology, paleoclimate, and geodynamics
Guoqing LinMGS Professor of Geophysics; Associate Dean of Research

Uses dense networks of seismic sensors to image what lies beneath Florida's coast and to study how ocean storms shake the solid Earth.

UM profile
Adam HoltMGS Associate Professor, Marine Geosciences

A geodynamicist who uses computer simulations to understand why Earth's tectonic plates move and how they deform where they meet.

UM profile
Amanda OehlertMGS Associate Professor, Marine Geosciences

A carbonate geologist who reads chemical signatures locked in seafloor sediments to reconstruct past environments, sea level, and the carbon cycle. Co-leads the Mozambique reef-core project with G. Eberli & R. Weger.

UM profile
Gregor EberliMGS Professor & Robert N. Ginsburg Endowed Chair, Marine Geosciences

Reads the sedimentary rock record to reconstruct ancient sea levels and environmental change across carbonate platforms. Co-leads the Mozambique reef-core project with A. Oehlert & R. Weger.

UM profile
Ralf WegerMGS Research Assistant Professor, Marine Geosciences

Measures the physical properties of carbonate rocks and sediment cores, linking what rocks are made of to how they formed. Co-leads the Mozambique reef-core project with G. Eberli & A. Oehlert.

UM profile
OCE

Ocean Sciences

Physical oceanography, biogeochemistry, and isotope geochemistry
Mariana BifOCE Assistant Professor, Ocean Sciences

Tracks how carbon and nitrogen cycle through the ocean using robotic BGC-Argo floats, satellites, and shipboard sampling. Runs the BIF Lab.

UM profile
Hilary CloseOCE Associate Professor, Ocean Sciences

A marine geochemist who follows the chemical fingerprints of carbon and nitrogen to map ocean food webs and the ocean's biological carbon pump.

UM profile
Franz Philip TuchenOCE Assistant Professor, Ocean Sciences

A sea-going physical oceanographer who studies tropical Atlantic currents and decades-long climate swings using long-term mooring observations.
